Diagnostic Wire Bead Analysis (Forensic Arson Differentiation)

CRITICAL CAUSE-ORIGIN EVIDENCE
Short-Circuit Arcing Beads (Primary Cause)

Formed by instantaneous localized high-voltage electrical arcing before fire eruption. Characterized by sharp demarcation boundaries between melted globule and intact conductor.

Thermal Flame Heat Beads (Consequential Effect)

Produced by external ambient fire heat after the blaze has commenced. Marked by gradual tapering, broad uniform oxide discoloration, and general conductor annealing.
